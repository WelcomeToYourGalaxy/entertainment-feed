# entertainment-feed

A live wire on the entertainment industries worldwide: who controls the
bottleneck, what decides which stories reach people, how much of their time it
takes, and how far into them the measurement now reaches.

Built after "The Entertainment Industries" on Welcome to Your Galaxy.

**This is a feed on the industry, not on its output.** Reviews, trailers,
casting, box-office weekends, awards, recaps and celebrity coverage are refused
— that is nearly all entertainment coverage, and none of it is the subject.

## On the neurotechnology claims

The section quotes figures for emotion-sensing accuracy and advertising return
that come from vendors and their own marketing, not from independent
evaluation. This wire carries the reporting and marks what has a measured
figure, but a company's claim about how well its own emotion recognition works
is a sales claim until someone else tests it. The neural and biometric subjects
are here because of what is being attempted and what data it produces, which is
documented — not because the accuracy claims are settled.

## Weight

A decision (2), institutional material (2), a measured figure (1), a pending
decision with a date (1), a named jurisdiction (1), a primary source (1). At
three or more it is marked consequential.

## Sources

184 wires, 30 direct. Like the science wire, the direct list is uneven for this
subject: the sibling repos carry privacy and rights organisations, which cover
the tracking and neurotechnology half well, but nothing on studios, labels,
streaming economics or games. The events block carries the industry half.
